# Courtyard tree canopies · 20 November 2026

**In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công · CONCEPT / UNSURVEYED LANDSCAPE.**

This is a preview of stage 5 of the [design excellence programme](../../docs/design-excellence.md#stage-5-preview-courtyard-tree-canopies). The 16 presentation trees keep their positions, trunks, branches, beds and edging, and their crowns are redrawn as leafy canopies.

The before captures are `440b545`. The after captures add `landscape-trees.js` to the sanctuary furnishings revision, which does not appear in these views. Both sets were rendered in headless desktop Chrome at 1600 × 900, in visit-only and presentation mode, through `church.render()`. The eleven Marian art textures on **PUBLICATION HOLD H8** were blanked during capture.

[Comparison](compare-trees.jpg)

Each crown fills the volume of the old cluster cloud. It has a dark inner mass and about 230 leaf-spray cards with alpha cut-outs. The cards cast shadows. From very close, the flat cards can show at the edge of the silhouette.

The species, size, planting, root zones and drainage are not designed. These trees are presentation context only, like the lawn.
