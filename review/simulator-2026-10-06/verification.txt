Corrections found by the checks in this round
- Four entrance projectors, the main-door exit sign and the two courtyard
  horns were mounted on surfaces the model does not have (a wall on axis 2′
  and the recessed central façade). They now sit on the façade's inner face
  (x 2.65 m) and on the tower fronts.
- The veranda ceiling was assumed flat at 6.0 m. The model's veranda roof
  slopes from about 7.15 m to 6.45 m, and the 9–10 wings have a gabled roof.
  Pendant anchors and the placement tool now follow the real roof.
- The entrance hall had no roof, so the sky and the backs of the shrines
  showed above the main door from inside. Added the +8.39 m terrace slab from
  the front elevation and the nave gable wall on axis 2′ above it (inferred
  from the elevations; confirm with CAD). The acoustic volume now includes
  the hall (7 456 m³).
- A veranda loudspeaker and a lantern hung at the same point. The lanterns now
  hang at the piers, the loudspeakers mid-bay in front of the wall openings.

Scope limits
All values are planning estimates for comparing options (simplified
point-source photometry, statistical room acoustics, empirical fan jets).
They are not certified lighting, acoustic, airflow or electrical design and
do not replace IES/LDT photometry, EASE/ODEON/CATT modelling, STIPA
measurement, the CBE fan tool or a full-scale trial. The entrance-bay
closure, the slatted panels and every product are proposals.
