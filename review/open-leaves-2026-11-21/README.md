# Open doors and windows · 21 November 2026

**In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công · CONCEPT.**

The owner asked that the windows and doors stand fully open, back against the walls, so that people walking along the veranda can see into the church. The change is recorded in the [design excellence programme](../../docs/design-excellence.md#open-doors-and-windows-21-november-2026) as DX-09.

Each image shows the **before** state on the left (`dd5f152`, served from a detached worktree) and the **after** state on the right (the same commit with this change). Both were rendered in headless desktop Chrome (Metal ANGLE) at 1600 × 900 and pixel ratio 1, through `church.render()`, with **Doors & windows → Open**, then reduced to 960 px per side. The eleven Marian art textures on **PUBLICATION HOLD H8** were blanked during capture, so the door reliefs and M lunettes show as plain stone.
