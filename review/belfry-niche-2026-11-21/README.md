# Central niche columns and belfry openings · 21 November 2026

**In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công.**

The owner made three observations on 21 November 2026:

- the two round columns beside the Assumption statue were missing;
- the tower columns were too faint and did not reach a base;
- the louvre timber looked flat, with the arched head floating above it.
