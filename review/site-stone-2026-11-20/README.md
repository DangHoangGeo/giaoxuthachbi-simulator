They were rendered in headless desktop Chrome at 1600 × 900, in visit-only and presentation mode, through `church.render()`. Headless Chrome's Metal graphics stopped rendering for part of this session (every material failed shader validation, even on an unchanged checkout). SwiftShader worked but took about 15 minutes per frame. These captures were made after the GPU path recovered.

The mortar reads darker in shadow than lime mortar would. The tone, the stone, the courtyard's extent, levels, drainage and slip resistance remain for the parish, the architect and the site design.
