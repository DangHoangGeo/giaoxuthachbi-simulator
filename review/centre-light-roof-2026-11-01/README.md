# Centre lighting and roof crossing · 1 November 2026

**Design development / not for construction.** The owner requested two-way light on the inside faces of the principal nave columns and clarified that the main ceiling and visible timber should continue through the wing crossing. The owner subsequently asked for a reasoned increase of the centre reading lights. The model now uses fourteen 800 lm / 8 W concept column lights on LD, 2,800 lm centre reading heads and 1,500 lm companion heads on the main tie beams. Side-beam heads remain 2,300 lm. The main interior lining and purlin proxies continue across the crossing; wing lining starts at the nave edge. Exterior roof geometry is unchanged.

The reproducible comparison is `node scripts/study_centre_lighting.cjs`. With four-block seating and simulated full service, the central seats at the 0.8 m book plane are 211–402 lux, mean 291; twelve centre-aisle samples at 1.2 m are 242–376 lux, mean 297. Under the same geometry, the previous 2,300/2,300 lm setting without column fixtures gave central seats 214–420 lux, mean 296, and aisle 209–337 lux, mean 261. A 4,600/2,300 lm double-output trial with 1,600 lm column fixtures gave central seats 239–582 lux, mean 428. These estimates use assumed optics and reflectance; they do not validate glare or establish measured lux.

Desktop visit-only views were inspected in day and evening, including two- and four-block seating and the sanctuary ceiling; the final visit-only console had no errors. Geometry, roof lining and fitting checks, column-emitter and saved-layout migration checks, electrical export, register-preservation workflow, model/plan, estimate and full simulator calculation audits were run. The full calculation audit reports 100% of sampled seats at 200 lux, central-seat mean 291 lux, but retains low ambo and altar feedback margins (0.9 and 1.4 dB), wing speech minimum 0.411 STI and ventilation/comfort limitations. The 200–300 lux central-seat mean is a project comparison target, not a claim of code compliance. The column fixtures, drivers, photometry, glare, fan flicker, fire/heat, timber fixings, cable approaches and maintenance access are unselected or unverified. The continuous visible roof is a visualization, not a verified continuous structural member: transition, supports, joints, loads, fire separation, roof ventilation, weathering and access remain **ENGINEERING HOLD** for the architect and structural and electrical designers. No site measurement or engineering approval has been represented.
