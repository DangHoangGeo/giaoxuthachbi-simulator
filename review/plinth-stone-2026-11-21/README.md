# Rock-faced stone base · 21 November 2026

**CONCEPT · In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công.**

The owner found the coursed-rubble plinth busy, "like a paper drawing", and asked for a simpler base that looks steady and strong, like rock. The same stone was then asked for on the outer faces of the side stairs and on the towers' lower columns.
