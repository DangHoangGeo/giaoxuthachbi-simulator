# Carved interior · model captures · 7 October 2026

Viewer screenshots after revision 5 of the [sanctuary model](../../docs/sanctuary-model.md) brought the 7 October interior concepts into the 3D model. These are actual model renders at commit `89a32c3`, not generated artwork and not site photographs. [capture.json](capture.json) records the cameras, which repeat those of the [earlier model captures](../church-views-2026-10-07/model-inputs/capture.json) so that before and after can be compared.

What the captures show: turned column bases on panelled stone pedestals, carved capitals, junction blocks and dies; lengthwise beams on the column lines; carved haunches, cartouches and tie-beam ends; gilded relief, spires and leaf crestings in the sanctuary; the glowing blue niches; the carved corpus and the gilded tabernacle.

What they do not show: the evening scene (checked in the browser on desktop and phone sizes, where the headless capture stalled), any measured light level, or any structural adequacy. The carving is generated geometry and pattern that approximates the art; the statues, shrine doors, altar front and the curved rail over the tie beams remain simpler than the concepts. See the limits in the sanctuary model document and the open coordination items in the [beam specification](../../docs/beams-roof-connections/SPECIFICATION.md).
