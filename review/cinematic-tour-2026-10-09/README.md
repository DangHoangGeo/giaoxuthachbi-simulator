# Cinematic tour · review record, 9 October 2026

The **Cinematic tour** button (Discover menu) replaces the earlier straight guided walk with a film of 5 min 08 s in 19 scenes: outside by day, inside by day, then the evening with the roof hidden. Organ music is generated by the viewer. Implementation: `Thach_Bi_Viewer/cinematic-tour.js`, `cinematic-tour.css` and three hooks in `bundle.js`. Use: `docs/simulator/guide.md`, section "Cinematic tour".

This is a display feature. It changes no dimension, equipment position, setting, schedule or calculation, so no drawing register, equipment register or summary report was affected.

## Not verified

- **How the music sounds.** Levels were measured; nobody has listened to it in this session. The piece, registration and reverberation are generated and may need adjusting by ear.
- **Full screen (F)** and **the notice shown when a browser withholds sound** were not exercised with a real key press.
- **Smoothness on slower computers.** The film was run on one Mac in the desktop app's browser pane. Automatic resolution stays active during the film.
- Phone layout: outside the current owner scope (desktop only).

## Limits to keep in mind

- The film shows the design-development model. Furnishings, ornament, lighting and sound equipment are proposals; the closing card says the design is not approved for construction.
- Screen brightness in the evening scenes is not a lux result, and the organ music is not an acoustic prediction: it is not played through the modelled loudspeakers or the room model.
- The flight path is fixed. After moving a fitting into the route (centre aisle below 4.3 m, 1.5–2 m off the centreline at 6.4–7.1 m, the side-B aisle at 2–2.4 m), run `CHURCH_CINEMA.audit()` again.
