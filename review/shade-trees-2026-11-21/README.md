# Courtyard shade trees · 21 November 2026

**In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công · CONCEPT / UNSURVEYED LANDSCAPE.**

The owner asked for much more realistic trees around the church. The 16 presentation trees are regrown as broad-leaved shade trees with forked, tapered trunks, curved limbs and lobed crowns of leaf clusters ([design record](../../docs/design-excellence.md#stage-5-preview-courtyard-tree-canopies)).

Each image shows the **before** state on the left (`dd5f152`, served from a detached worktree; the canopies of [20 November](../tree-canopies-2026-11-20/README.md)) and the **after** state on the right (the same commit with this change). Both were rendered in headless desktop Chrome (Metal ANGLE) at 1600 × 900 and pixel ratio 1, by day, through `church.render()`, then reduced to 960 px per side. The eleven Marian art textures on **PUBLICATION HOLD H8** were blanked during capture.
