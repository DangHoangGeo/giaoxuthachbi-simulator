# Short ramp near the H tower · 21 November 2026

**In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công · CONCEPT / ENGINEERING HOLD.**

At the owner's request, the five-run switchback ramp is replaced by one short ramp near the tower. It follows the P05 curve from the forecourt, runs along the tower and lands at the tower rear on the lower courtyard. It has an outside handrail only.
