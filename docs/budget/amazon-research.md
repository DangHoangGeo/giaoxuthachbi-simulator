## Coverage and open questions

The current machine count is 326 scheduled items plus 5 distribution/control enclosures, with 15 hidden alternatives and 343 routes. The reported inventory includes 20 shown non-electrical/enclosure entries. These 11 comparisons cover eight equipment type IDs and two infrastructure units; prices are not multiplied across equipment quantities here. The parent budget schedule determines quantities and excludes hidden alternatives from its base subtotal. It must also exclude the explicitly 100 V comparisons from an estimate for a 220–240 V supply and must not use the size-mismatched Panasonic fan prices as project fan unit prices.

The modeled lighting types still needing a well-matched Amazon Japan candidate or a documented no-price reason are `servicePanel`, `projector36`, `projector24`, `chandelier8`, `chandelier12`, `sconce2`, `uplight`, `spot15`, `lantern`, `wallLantern`, `exitSign`, `corniceFlood`, and `lanternString`. The current generic floodlight and household chandelier must not be used as proxies for those different optics, architectural fixtures, emergency equipment, or decorative forms. No LED tape/strip type is present in the model. The string-light model is a festoon string with bulbs, not LED tape.

The modeled air types not covered by a reference are `fanCeiling` and `fanWallLarge`. No comparison establishes the project’s no-visible-fan central-view constraint, mounting loads, airflow at operating duty, occupied-zone comfort, or noise during worship. The Panasonic exhaust fan is 100 mm rather than the modeled 500 mm. The KODEN wall fan matches the 450 mm diameter but is explicitly 100 V and lacks airflow, noise, and mounting data; do not use its price in a 220–240 V procurement subtotal.

The modeled sound types `slimColumn` and `horn`, plus the `mic` type, have Japanese-maker comparison prices. The Amazon search for [TOA PE-304 30 W pendant speaker](https://www.amazon.co.jp/s?k=TOA+PE-304+30W+pendant+speaker) returned a 3 W in-ceiling TOA speaker and the 5 W Panasonic horn, but no matching 30 W pendant product page; `pendantSpeaker` remains unpriced. Passive-speaker ratings and impedance taps are audio-system values, not mains demand. The source equipment model does not include modeled amplifier or mixer quantities, so amp/mixer racks and AV power peripherals remain allowances outside an equipment-based price extension unless the parent budget explicitly maps a separate allowance. No unmodeled AV hardware is added to a material subtotal by these references.

The modeled `servicePanel` entries are planning luminaire panels; the separate five board enclosures still need a credible enclosure/board comparison only if suitable product pages expose pack price and ratings. Cable/conduit product prices are infrastructure comparisons, not project selections. Route lengths do not establish conductor lengths or installed allowances: cable size, conductor count, voltage drop, derating, circuit loading, containment, local code and waste/spare factors remain open. Do not multiply a domestic 100 m cable pack or 50 m conduit roll across the modeled route metres as if it were a take-off.

Amazon listings reviewed here do not establish intended service life, long-term spare-parts availability in Vietnam, warranty support there, or suitability for the humid/coastal service environment. Obtain manufacturer data and local supplier confirmation for the selected products before a life-cycle budget or procurement schedule is relied on. Final voltage, earthing, phases, fault level, protection, cable sizing, ingress/exposure ratings, photometry, fan duty/noise, loudspeaker directivity and mounting, and coordinated installation details remain engineering inputs. This comparison work is not construction approval or readiness to purchase.
