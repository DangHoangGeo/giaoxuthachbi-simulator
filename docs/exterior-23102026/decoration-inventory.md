## Evidence and coordinate conventions

The project basis is the 23 October 2026 DWG, SHA-256 `2dc33f5dd0f9aa07cf9cf8b7867a55e214caf2d042df3b3c5dd9eebb48f7d4ae`. The [exterior review](README.md) and its linked witness JSON files retain handles, source endpoints, transforms and residuals. The [façade record](../facade-model.md) identifies E01B (CAD sheet 1), PT10/PT15, `facade.csv` and the workbook **DWG facade** sheet. Coordinates in the model are metres, X from axis 1 toward sanctuary, Y up from nave floor, Z from D/E midpoint toward H. CAD dimensions have been interpreted as mm; `INSUNITS=1` conflicts with that interpretation (ISSUE-R06). The local PDF guide prohibits scaling its pages. Do not infer depth from elevation or describe software residuals as survey accuracy.

The local, unpublished PDF volume 11 has 25 pages of enlarged vector details: index on p. 2; T01–T14 on pp. 3–16 (E01B front except T14 E02 side); T15/PT15A and T16/PT29 plans on pp. 17–18; T17–T20/E02 on pp. 19–22; T21–T23/untitled ET on pp. 23–25. These identifiers are navigation aids to the CAD, not independent verification or publication permission. ET's orientation and the stage-2 rose discrepancy remain ENG-040; the twelve inside/rear upper faces are derived repetitions, not independently verified. Drawings and overlays remain local under H1.

## Shared interface and limitations

[`exterior-ornament.js`](../../Thach_Bi_Viewer/exterior-ornament.js) exports `CHURCH_EXTERIOR_ORNAMENT.create(THREE, carvingKit)`, returning `fleur(height)`, `pilasterCapital(width,height)`, `meanderGeo(length,height)` and `spray(height)`. Arguments are metres. These return geometry facing local +Z, with the baseline at local Y=0; `meanderGeo` runs along +X from X=0. Placement, mirroring, material, mesh name and source/status metadata remain with each caller. The capital cache belongs to each factory instance; geometry can be shared by its callers and must not be disposed while in use. These generators intentionally preserve the earlier illustrative shapes and are not certified CAD profiles. `carving.js` remains the lower-level kit. No new source-derived motif has yet been claimed complete.

The original tower implementation was moved without changing its generator bodies; the façade's matching duplicated fleur/capital bodies now use the same factory. `roofline.js` now depends on the shared module directly. HTML, model/check loaders and the legacy export loader load it before the consumers; web visit builds copy it with the viewer. Instance names, placements, equipment, routes, simulator physics and user layouts are unchanged. Existing PDF exports documented as stale in [towers](towers.md) remain stale; no new geometry export is required for this extraction. No equipment or route change occurred, so category registers and the electrical summary do not need regeneration. Relevant governing records checked: [exterior review](README.md), [façade model](../facade-model.md), [tower balcony](tower-balcony.md), [rear and roofline](rear-and-roofline.md), and [electrical controls](../electrical-grid/controls.md).

Next, inspect the actual CAD entities and enlarged detail sheets for one motif family at a time, record exact source endpoints and discrepancies, then replace its proxy in a separate reviewed change. All structural, electrical, maintenance, sightline and central-view holds continue to apply.

## Extraction verification

The four shared generator bodies were compared byte-for-byte with their pre-extraction tower implementations; the two matching façade implementations were also compared. `verify_exterior_sources.py` (217 witness records), `verify_exterior_dwg.cjs`, `verify_tower_balcony.cjs`, `verify_model.cjs --plan`, `verify_construction.cjs`, `verify_simulator.cjs --electrical`, and `verify_simulator.cjs --report --estimates` passed their software checks. The latter still reports 0.9/1.4 dB microphone feedback margins and wing STI minimum 0.410 / average 0.484 below the design target; 61% of sampled seats reach the modelled preferred air-speed range. Web tests (116), typecheck, production build, viewer-copy and build-boundary checks passed; the build reported a known vulnerability in the installed Next.js version. Desktop inspection compared the front façade before and after in daylight, inspected the evening view and visit-only façade, confirmed the Simulator button was absent in visit mode, and found no visit-mode console errors. No screenshot was retained for this extraction; this visual check does not establish detailed CAD conformity. Existing acoustic, lighting, ventilation and sightline shortcomings remain; these checks are not engineering approval.

The first motif selected for detailed work is recorded in the [tower-front spandrel review](tower-spandrel-review.md); the owner confirmed scoped reproduction permission, and the two H-front outlines are now transcribed as recorded there; 3D relief and B-front source confirmation remain open.
