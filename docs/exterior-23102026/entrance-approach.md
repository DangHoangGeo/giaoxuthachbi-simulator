## Accessibility review required

[QCVN 10:2024/BXD, Ministry of Construction](https://moc.gov.vn/Images/editor/files/Quy%20Chu%E1%BA%A9n/BXD_06-2024-TT-BXD_01082024.pdf), clauses 2.2.3–2.2.5, provides a basis for the responsible designer to assess ramps, landings, handrails and entrance steps. In particular, the 160 mm inferred riser exceeds the 150 mm entrance-step maximum in 2.2.5 if that provision applies. Do not resolve this by silently changing source geometry or presenting these steps as compliant. The 230 mm model tread on the separate three-riser entrance flight also needs review against the same clause's 300 mm minimum. Confirm applicability, source section and measured levels with the architect and authority; design the lower and upper accessible connections, turn/landing geometry, cross slopes, grip surfaces, drainage, edge protection, continuous handrails, illumination, evacuation and maintenance. Confirm all clearances and clash resolution on site. Until then the modeled wheelchair path is incomplete and unsuitable for construction.
