After seeing the tall solid support of the shorter alternative, the owner requested exploration of a compact switchback. The current **concept** uses the P05 curve and five 1.400 m wide switchback runs. The switchback expands outward on the H side from Z +13.722 to +21.622 m. It is an interpretation for review, not a drawing-shown or surveyed design. The inner P05 edge is offset 0.180 m from the source to clear the current tower plinth in plan.

The path links the model's lower courtyard (−2.080 m) to the forecourt (−0.480 m) using about 22.248 m of modelled sloping travel at approximately 1:13.9, with level turns. These levels, 1.400 m run widths, terrain and support infill are assumptions. A tower-side ramp cannot by itself connect the forecourt to the church floor at ±0. The outer handrail is shown only on the exposed upper curve and far outside edge as requested; these sections are **not continuous**, and the switchback has unprotected internal edges and level differences. This is a **CONCEPT / ENGINEERING HOLD**, not a safe, complete or approved wheelchair route. The previous foreground railings blocked the side stair returns and were removed; their coordinated replacement remains open.

## Accessibility review required

[QCVN 10:2024/BXD, Ministry of Construction](https://moc.gov.vn/Images/editor/files/Quy%20Chu%E1%BA%A9n/BXD_06-2024-TT-BXD_01082024.pdf), clauses 2.2.3–2.2.5, provides a basis for the responsible designer to assess ramps, landings, handrails and entrance steps. Clause 2.2.4 calls for continuous handrails on both sides of a ramp; the owner-requested outer-only display therefore requires resolution with the designer and authority if that clause applies. In addition, the 160 mm inferred riser exceeds the 150 mm entrance-step maximum in 2.2.5 if that provision applies. Do not resolve this by silently changing source geometry or presenting these steps as compliant. The 230 mm model tread on the separate three-riser entrance flight also needs review against the same clause's 300 mm minimum. Confirm applicability, source section and measured levels with the architect and authority; design the lower and upper accessible connections, turn/landing geometry, cross slopes, grip surfaces, drainage, edge protection, continuous handrails, illumination, evacuation and maintenance. Confirm all clearances and clash resolution on site. Until then the modeled wheelchair path is incomplete and unsuitable for construction.
