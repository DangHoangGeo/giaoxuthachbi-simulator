# Exterior decoration improvement plan

**Planning only · In development / Đang phát triển · Not for construction / Không dùng để thi công.** No exterior component or model change is authorized by this plan alone. Work proceeds one reviewed part at a time, starting with reusable decoration components. The owner's goal is to reproduce the new design in detail; a component is only described as matching a detail when the available evidence supports that claim.

## Design basis and present state

The owner selected the 23 October 2026 DWG as the project design basis. Use the actual drawing, its printed sheet/drawing identifier, handles and existing witness records, with the [exterior review](README.md) and [façade model record](../facade-model.md). The local 23102026 PDF set includes volume 11, enlarged tower decoration details; its guide says not to measure from the PDF and records the unresolved insertion-unit conflict. Designer drawings and derivative overlays remain local while publication hold H1 applies. The [reference images](../../Thach_Bi_Viewer/references/03-facade/README.md) are concept views, not evidence of hidden geometry or dimensions.

Existing building blocks are not a blank slate: `carving.js` has general carving generators; `towers.js` exposes `CHURCH_TOWERS.ornament` (fleur, capital, meander and spray), which `roofline.js` already uses; `facade.js` has its own use of shared carving. Several profiles, capitals, tracery, relief depths and connections remain illustrative. First inventory their actual call sites and output before proposing a shared interface; preserve existing appearance and IDs during extraction.

## Sequence and review gates

Each phase ends in a small, coherent, checked local commit with its source and documentation updates. Review the completed part against its references before starting the next part; do not claim the entire exterior is exact after checking one view. If a decision or source detail is missing, record it as a hold and continue only independent work.

1. **Inventory and evidence.** Catalogue every visible ornament by location and repeated motif: mouldings and cornices, dentils and meanders, pilaster bases/flutes/capitals, roses and tracery, foliate sprays and cresting, pinnacles/fleurs, niche surrounds, balustrades and crosses. For each, record existing module and object names, occurrences and orientation; source revision, printed sheet and detail/handle or workbook cell; dimensions, endpoints and units; current status and gaps. Inspect the enlarged tower-detail sheets and actual CAD where available. Separate drawing-shown outlines from derived repetition and unknown depth, material and attachment. Preserve the unit conflict ISSUE-R06 and all source discrepancies.
2. **Reusable component foundation — first implementation.** Extract or adapt the existing generators into a dedicated, offline-compatible exterior ornament module rather than duplicating them in each facade. Define a small documented interface in metres with local origin, facing/handedness, dimensions, material input and optional level of detail; use distinct named groups/meshes, stable instance identifiers and source/status metadata. Separate reusable geometry from placement, support surfaces and equipment; allow genuinely different drawn variants rather than forcing one universal pattern. Reuse geometry/materials where safe, dispose resources correctly and avoid changing analytical geometry. Integrate with the current script loading order and existing users without breaking offline or `?visit`. First verify that this extraction preserves the current appearance, positions and behaviour; only then improve individual components.
3. **Component-by-component detail.** In a separate reviewable change for each motif family, transcribe the supported silhouette, proportions, repetition and profiles from the applicable new drawing. Build left/right or face variants when the source warrants them; provide close, front and oblique inspection views and a repeatable source-to-model comparison. Keep an explicit `CONCEPT` proxy wherever relief, rear face or profile is not established; do not invent a depth and call it exact. Catalogue each component's evidence, parameters, instances and unresolved questions before deploying it across the church.
4. **Towers.** Apply reviewed components one storey and face at a time: lower bases and blind arches; shafts, capitals and entablatures; upper openings and roses; balcony and pinnacles; dome, lantern and cross. Review the B and H towers and their front, outer, inner and rear faces independently. ET orientation and the 90 mm rose discrepancy remain ENG-040; derived rear/inner repetition is not a confirmed drawing. Preserve ENG-041's balcony and light-occlusion hold and the existing ground-floor wall-plane discrepancy.
5. **Central front.** Work from the ground-storey entrances and pilasters upward through the great arches, windows and roses, niche surrounds and statues, upper cornices, pinnacles and crown. Respect the current axis-2 entrance, steps, exit sign and lighting; resolve ENG-039 through coordinated design before changing the door/sign arrangement. Verify the niche sizes, weather exposure and ENG-036 ventilation hold rather than using ornament to conceal them.
6. **Side elevations and wing ends.** Review both long sides, windows and door surrounds, porch parapets and repeated piers, then each transverse gable and its roses, niches, raking cornices and cresting. Compare both sides separately; do not assume the unresolved B/H orientation or asymmetrical equipment is mirrored. Keep door/exit and access conflicts visible.
7. **Sanctuary end and roofline.** Complete rear piers, gable, rose, cornices, pinnacles, cross and their roof junctions, then inspect the whole roofline from both sides. Keep the rear-plane discrepancy and ISSUE-R05 ridge conflict visible; do not infer waterproofing, supports or drainage from the model.
8. **Whole-exterior reconciliation.** Compare the front, both sides, rear, roofline and tower details as one revision, including human-eye and close views in day and evening. Identify any remaining proxy, mismatch, stale export or missing source in the owning records. Refresh affected generated outputs and review evidence; identify what needs architect, structural, electrical or other specialist decisions. Completion means the planned modelling review is recorded, not that construction approval or full exactness has been established.

## Acceptance for every component and area

- Record source revision, sheet/detail or CAD handle, units, endpoints, transform, model version, tolerances and confidence. Use existing witness files where applicable. A drawn 2D outline does not establish its 3D depth, material, hidden rear or attachment. Label `DRAWING SHOWN`, `MODEL TRANSCRIPTION`, `DERIVED`, `CONCEPT` and `ENGINEERING HOLD` accurately; ask the responsible designer about missing information.
- Check contact, openings, sightlines, maintenance and equipment clearances, light/sound/air paths, weather and roof interfaces, structural loads, accessibility and electrical routing. Preserve the hidden-fan constraint and existing unresolved engineering holds. Never move equipment or alter calculations to make a decorative change pass.
- Preserve stable model and equipment IDs, user layouts, offline use and rendering performance. If equipment, aiming or routes change, coordinate the model, controls, electrical exports, category Excel registers and summary from the same revision; preserve entered data and retired IDs. Record when these are unaffected and why.
- Update the owning exterior/engineering records and regenerate affected plan, construction and other exports. Mark old PDF or image exports stale when necessary. Keep copyrighted drawing derivatives local until publication permission is resolved.
- Run applicable source and geometry checks from [the exterior review](README.md), `node scripts/verify_model.cjs --plan`, `node scripts/verify_construction.cjs`, simulator/electrical and web checks when affected, and `git diff --check`. Check the real desktop viewer in day and evening, relevant close/full views and `OPEN_CHURCH.html?visit`, including console errors. Record the exact checks, screenshots permitted for publication, failures and unverified conditions; software success is not engineering approval.

## First deliverable after this plan

Produce the inventory and evidence map, then a small appearance-preserving shared-component extraction with before/after desktop evidence and focused regression checks. Do not begin replacing tower or facade ornament until that foundation has been reviewed. The exact scope of each later component and area depends on what the drawing actually establishes.
