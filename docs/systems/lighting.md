## Rendering correction

The former renderer combined neighbouring lights at an average location when the graphics budget was exceeded. For example, combining path lights at axes 4 and 6 placed the virtual source near the speaker at axis 5. It also changed the average beam direction and output, making light appear to come from speakers and leaving tower faces dark.

Every switched-on emitter supplies both diffuse light and physical material reflections at every distance. The lamp texture calls Three.js `RE_Direct` just like native lights, including Fresnel energy conservation, GGX specular, clearcoat and sheen. Each real emitter is evaluated once. This replaces the earlier diffuse-only fallback, which made reflections appear when a light received a native slot near the camera.

The two shadow slots remain on the first two visible fittings configured to cast shadows (the paired altar key lights in the recommended design), at every quality level and camera distance. An off lamp leaves its own shadow slot empty; it does not transfer it to another fitting. Room bounce is evaluated by each surface's world position inside the nave or wings; entering the church or hiding the roof no longer changes ambient brightness across the whole model. Fixed exposure is restored once for saved layouts, while optional automatic eye adaptation remains available.

**Simulator → Settings → Rendering quality** changes the native/texture split. All lamps retain their illumination and reflections, with the same shadow sources. A separate **View settings → Preview resolution** control now adapts framebuffer size on slow hardware; manual full resolution remains available. Explicit light graphics disables antialiasing and preview shadow maps. Conservative spatial light lists skip only noncontributing spots, with no brightness or distance threshold. See [performance methods and checks](../simulator/performance.md). Additional sources do not gain shadow maps. This is approximate real-time lighting, without measured manufacturer photometry or ray-traced occlusion for every lamp. The analysis continues to use all configured sources.

## Verification

`node scripts/verify_simulator.cjs --report --export-electrical` checks pairs at each modeled side doorway, only one front decorative pair, mirrored tower placements and aims, wall mounts, cornice support, projecting head clearance, real emitter origins at balanced/fast settings, unchanged canvas resolution, fixed shadow identities and ambient brightness across near/far cameras and every quality setting, front-surface coverage from the pedestal to the crown, complete source coverage from near/far and interior/exterior views, remote switch/dimmer updates, connected routes, selection and reversible systems isolation. Existing lighting, audio, airflow, history and import/export checks also run. The GPU regression page in `review/stable-interior-2026-10-06/gpu-check.html` compares actual native/texture shading of plaster, wood, metal, clearcoat and sheen at near/far camera distances.

The full-service calculation after this review gives approximately **346 lux average**, **193 lux minimum**, and **99% of seats at or above 200 lux**, with about **5.51 kW** total running load. These are simulator estimates. The 3D preview and calculations do not replace manufacturer photometry or a construction lighting design.
