## Coupled calculation scope

Use unchanged code and identical receivers for option comparisons until a documented method correction is independently validated. A material method change requires rerunning both baseline and candidates. Preserve unsuccessful candidates and missing values. Parameter sensitivities describe the current approximation, not measured uncertainty confidence intervals.

| Method | Existing scope | Evidence needed before engineering prediction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Analytic catalogue emission, model occlusion, approximate room indirect component, maintenance factor. Books at local floor +0.80 m; fixture rendering is separate. | Selected IES/LDT, actual output/driver/thermal limits, finishes and maintenance basis; vertical faces, glare, daylight and blade interaction. |
| Sound | Approximate frequency bands/directivity, diffuse room absorption, arrival delay/echo, STI-like speech estimate, fan noise and simplified feedback margin. | Manufacturer data, occupied/unoccupied RT/background survey, actual mic/talker geometry, room coupling and calibrated STIPA/level/feedback commissioning. |
| Air | Empirical ceiling/wall jet and oscillation-duty approximations combined by root-sum-square. Occupied seat air sampled at +0.60 m. Exhaust ACH sums catalogue free-air flows. | Fan curves and installed loss network; intake free area and weather operation; pressure/flow balance and recirculation; ankle/torso/head and time-varying measurements, comfort and heat assessment. |
| Geometry/visibility | Displayed model is more detailed than the analytical occluders. Current point sightlines are partial studies. | Provenance for all relevant obstructions and openings; full equipment-envelope visibility, occupant postures, maintenance clearances and access mock-ups. |
| Electrical/control | Logical circuits, routes and scenes; nominal operating/rated estimates, proposed boards and corridors. | Nameplates, phase allocation, supply/fault/earth tests, protection/cable calculations and approved schematics; physical command/feedback/failure commissioning. |

Do not treat `openings=0` as a sealed-building air-flow simulation: current scalar changes acoustic room assumptions, while physical opening geometry and exhaust-rating flow remain separate. Day/evening rendering does not compute a solar/daylight design. Headless runs cannot establish concealment or usability. See [methods and limitations](../simulator/methods-and-limitations.md).
