## Scenarios

[Scenario inputs](../../review/engineering-baseline-2026-10-08/study-inputs/scenarios.json) freeze 24 cases: eight current scenes in each 2-/4-block layout, and eight one-variable/paired environmental or usage sensitivities on the four-block full-service case. Every case records all resulting equipment and settings. Defaults: occupancy .60, openings 1, mixed roof/slatted entrance finish, 28 °C, 75% RH, 40 dBA ambient, .80 maintenance, .40 m talker distance and 62 dBA talker level. Those are simulator assumptions, not field data.

Sensitivities are **CONCEPT inputs**: 35 °C/60% RH hot case; 30 °C/90% RH/.25 opening wet case; 20 °C/70% RH cool case; occupancy 1 and .10; opening scalar 0; ambient 50 dBA; talker distance .60 m. These probe model behavior without implying local design weather or approved operation. The model has no outdoor wind variable: “hot-still” does not simulate natural ventilation. Keep this omission visible.

The following are mandatory design cases but **not numerically resolved** by the seat export: daylight/solar gain and glare; measured rain-driven opening restrictions; dedicated choir and accessible positions; outdoor overflow receiver grid and neighbours; emergency evacuation/supply; single fan/amplifier/circuit loss, feeder-open, sensor/controller/network failure and restart. E2/E3/E5 must add fit-for-purpose evidence; listing a case is not passing it.

## Viewpoint and human coverage for concealment

E3 must freeze coordinates, directions, field of view, local floor height, visible equipment envelopes and screenshot/ray evidence for both seating layouts, with geometry source revision. Proposed sampling heights below are study assumptions, not claimed anthropometric percentiles; verify in parish mock-ups.

| Entrances/exterior | Central/side doors, verandas, courtyard arrival and approach boundaries; inspect towers, intakes/exhausts and board access | Shorter/seated/standing sweep with surveyed ground levels |
| Service | Isolation, opening panel, driver/filter/fan replacement and cleaning access | Actual maintainer/task envelope; intentionally accessible panels are a separate service state |

Light-beam clearance, speaker radiation through the concealment, fan inlet/outlet free area, thermal dissipation, movement and replacement access must be assessed at the same time. A hidden mesh toggle, an opaque layer absent from physics, or a line ray to an object's centre cannot close V-01. Existing structural/egress and equipment safety holds take precedence over visual preferences; conflicts remain open for a documented decision.
