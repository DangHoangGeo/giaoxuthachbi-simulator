## Requested evidence, in practical order

1. **Current site package:** surveyed address/levels/grid/openings, actual construction state and approved drawings; roof/cornice/service-room/DB access sections, substrates and fire boundaries. Include permit/approval authority and intended occupancy/accessible/choir locations. Resolves ENG-001/008–015.

3. **Design decisions:** occupied-seat/service and seasonal scenarios, adopted light/STI/feedback/noise/comfort criteria, concealment trade-offs and required local manual/group controls; budget/horizon and maintenance owner. Resolves ENG-007/011/014/018. Existing brief targets stay visible while decisions are pending.
4. **Measurements:** supply/fault/earth by qualified electrical team; opening/free-area/weather observations and fan duty tests; occupied/unoccupied RT/background and STIPA; maintained lux/glare and full-scale concealed-equipment mock-ups. Record calibration, uncertainty, location, date, scenario and responsible verifier.
