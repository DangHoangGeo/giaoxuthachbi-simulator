# Code of conduct

This project serves a parish community and depends on volunteers with very different backgrounds: engineers, builders, parishioners and software developers, writing in English or Vietnamese. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and reviews is asked to follow this code.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here write in a second language or are new to GitHub.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Explain what is wrong and how you know.
- Be honest about uncertainty. Say when something is an estimate, an opinion or outside your expertise.
- Respect the church, the parish and its faith, whatever your own beliefs.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal details or images without their consent.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or mockery of a person, their language, religion or background.
- Publishing private information about anyone.
- Presenting unchecked work as approved engineering, or pressing the parish to buy a product or service.
- Spam and advertising.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@DangHoangGeo](https://github.com/DangHoangGeo), privately through GitHub. For content that should be removed quickly, use GitHub's own “Report content” option on the comment. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions and block accounts that do not follow this code.
