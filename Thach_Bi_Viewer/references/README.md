## Why older images remain

The other discipline folders contain source photographs, approved appearance concepts, timber connection studies and earlier whole-church references. They are retained for traceability and ongoing design work. Their presence does not make an older design current; some earlier views show superseded shutters, sanctuary decoration or facade iconography. The reference library labels this material separately below the latest gallery.

Rejected or broken generation candidates were not copied into these reference folders. Keep original sources and native masters intact. Follow the [design notes](../../docs/church-view-renderings.md) and recorded source revisions; generated artwork cannot establish dimensions, structural capacity or construction approval.
