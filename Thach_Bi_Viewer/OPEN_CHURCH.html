Feel your way around

01

Explore the outside

Drag to orbit around the church. Scroll or pinch to move closer. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose Front facade for the corrected space between the towers.

02

Walk inside

Choose Go inside. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to move, and drag anywhere in the scene to look around: the scene follows your pointer, as it does outside. On touch screens, move with the round control.

03

Choose your next stop

Select a place on the left or click the minimap. Cinematic tour plays a five-minute film around and through the church, ending at night with the roof hidden. Its organ music is generated by the viewer: a homeland theme outside and the Bach–Gounod “Ave Maria” inside. Save as video in the film records it as a video file. Space pauses, ← → change scene, M switches the music, F fills the screen and Esc stops.

04

Study a detail

Open View settings for close-ups, day/evening light, roof visibility, and drawing views. In Explore mode, Alt-click a surface to read its source note. Esc leaves Walk mode.

Navigation stays at human eye height on the modeled floors and avoids walls, columns, and furniture. Stair shapes and navigation boundaries are illustrative.

Sources and model notes

The supplied architectural PDFs control the measured grids, levels, and building form. The companion Excel workbook preserves the explicit dimensions and unresolved drawing differences. Newly generated images and interior decorations are visual proposals.
